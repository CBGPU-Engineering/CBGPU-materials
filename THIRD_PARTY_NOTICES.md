CBGPU Materials is Apache-2.0. Rust dependencies include Axum, Tokio, Hyper,
Reqwest, rustls, Serde, UUID, tower-http, fs2 and tempfile. Their exact versions
and transitive dependencies are recorded in Cargo.lock. Each dependency keeps
its own license; consult its crate metadata when redistributing binaries.

The retired Python service used FastAPI, Starlette, Uvicorn and HTTPX. Its source
and notices remain available in the Git history preceding the Rust replacement.
